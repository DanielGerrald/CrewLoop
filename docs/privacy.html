Privacy Policy for Ohmly

Last updated: September 2026

Ohmly is a demonstration app built as a personal software engineering portfolio project. It is not a commercial product and is not distributed to the general public for operational use.

Data Collection

Ohmly does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data shown in the app (job records, names, addresses, contacts) is sample/mock data created for demonstration purposes only.

Device Permissions

The app may request access to your device's camera and photo library solely to demonstrate document and photo upload workflows within the app. Any photos selected or captured are used only within the local app session and are not uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

Ohmly does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
