Privacy Policy for Ohmly

Last updated: September 2026

Ohmly is a demonstration app built as a personal software engineering portfolio project. It is not a commercial product and is not distributed to the general public for operational use.

Data Collection and Storage

Ohmly uses a private Google Firebase project as its backend, accessible only to the developer. The app currently supports a single demonstration user account rather than multiple end-user accounts. The following data is stored in Firebase:

  • Photos and documents: Images and files captured, selected, or uploaded within the app (e.g., before/after job photos, supporting documents) are uploaded to Firebase Storage.
  • Work order and checkout form data: Text entered into job forms (e.g., service descriptions, material notes, sign-off names, work log entries) is stored in Firebase's database.

This data is used solely to demonstrate the app's functionality and is not sold, shared with third parties or advertisers, and is not used for tracking. Sample/demo data used in screenshots and reviewer testing is not real customer or job-site information.

Device Permissions

The app requests access to your device's camera, photo library, and file system to support photo and document upload features.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored in Firebase is retained for as long as needed to support the app's demonstration purposes. To request deletion of any data associated with your use of the app, contact the email below.

Third-Party Services

Ohmly uses Google Firebase (a Google service) for backend storage. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Ohmly does not integrate with any advertising or analytics/tracking SDKs.
